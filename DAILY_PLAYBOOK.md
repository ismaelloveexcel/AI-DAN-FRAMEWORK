# AIDAN Daily Playbook

> Open this every morning. Read it once. Then do the ONE thing.

---

## How to Start Your Day

1. Open `dashboard/index.html` in your browser (or your deployed Vercel URL)
2. Check the **Today** tab — AIDAN has your ONE task
3. Do the task (max 2–3 hours)
4. Mark it complete
5. Close laptop

That's it. That's the whole day.

---

## If You Need AIDAN

1. Go to ⚙️ **AIDAN** tab in the dashboard
2. Click **"Copy System Prompt"**
3. Paste into ChatGPT (GPT-4o) or Cursor
4. Ask your question
5. Paste key takeaway back into dashboard notes

---

## If You Have a New Idea

1. Stop. Write it in the **Ideas** tab (status: Idea)
2. Do NOT switch to it today
3. On Sunday, review ideas with AIDAN
4. AIDAN will evaluate it against the 5 Kill Criteria
5. If it passes: add it to the pipeline queue

**Rule: ONE product until $500 MRR.**

---

## Weekly Ritual (Every Sunday, 30 min)

1. Check 📊 **Revenue** tab
2. Update MRR and User numbers (get from Airtable)
3. Open AIDAN tab → ask for weekly review (paste system prompt into ChatGPT)
4. Approve or reject the ONE improvement suggestion
5. Update next week's task in the **Today** tab
6. That's it — close everything

---

## The Build Rules

- Max **3 days** to build anything
- If it takes longer → scope is wrong, not your speed
- One feature at a time
- Deploy before it's perfect
- Fix based on real user feedback, not assumptions

---

## The Financial Rules

- Do NOT spend money before you have revenue
- Rs 0 marketing spend until you have proof people want it
- Free tools only until $500 MRR
- When you hit $500 MRR → then you can spend to grow

---

## The Life Rules

- Max **3 hours of work per day** on this
- If you're working more than 3 hours → you're overcomplicating it
- Weekends are for review, not building
- Celebrate every milestone, no matter how small
- A first paying customer beats 100 features

---

## Emergency Protocol (When Stuck)

If you haven't moved in 48 hours:

1. Ask AIDAN: "I'm stuck on [X]. What's the simplest way to make progress today?"
2. Do exactly what it says
3. Don't overthink the answer

---

## The Non-Negotiable Rules

```
Revenue > Features
Execution > Planning
Speed > Perfection
Consistency > Intensity
$500 MRR > Everything else
```
