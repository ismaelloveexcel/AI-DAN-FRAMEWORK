# AI Excel Report Generator

## One-Liner
Upload a messy spreadsheet → get a clean formatted report with charts, insights, and recommendations in under 60 seconds.

---

## Product Brief

### Target User
- Mauritius SMBs (small/medium businesses): accountants, finance managers, business owners
- Anyone who spends hours formatting Excel reports manually
- Managers who need monthly reports but have no analyst

### The Problem
Business owners and finance teams in Mauritius spend hours every week:
- Manually formatting raw data into presentable reports
- Creating charts from scratch
- Writing the "so what" narrative around numbers
- Rebuilding the same structure every month

### The Solution
Upload any messy spreadsheet → AI cleans it, formats it, generates charts, and writes an executive summary with key insights and recommendations.

**Output includes:**
- Clean formatted table
- 2–3 auto-generated charts (bar, line, pie as appropriate)
- Key insights in bullet points ("Sales dropped 23% in March — likely linked to...")
- Recommendations ("Consider reviewing pricing for the following SKUs...")
- Downloadable as PDF or Excel
