# Small Business AI Assistant

## One-Liner
WhatsApp or web chatbot that answers Mauritius SMB questions on business registration, pricing, compliance, Facebook ads, and daily operations — like a business advisor in your pocket.

---

## Product Brief

### Target User
- Mauritius small business owners: shops, restaurants, freelancers, market stalls
- First-time entrepreneurs navigating Mauritius business registration
- SMBs with no access to professional advisors (accountants, lawyers, marketers)

### The Problem
Running a small business in Mauritius is hard without guidance:
- Business registration process is confusing (MRA, Registrar of Companies)
- VAT rules change and most SMBs don't understand them
- Facebook advertising is increasingly important but poorly understood
- No affordable way to ask "quick questions" without hiring a consultant

### The Solution
An AI chatbot (WhatsApp or web) that knows Mauritius-specific business rules and answers practical questions instantly:

**Example questions it handles:**
- "How do I register as a sole trader in Mauritius?"
- "What's the current VAT rate and when do I need to register?"
- "How much should I charge for [service] in Mauritius?"
- "How do I run a Facebook ad for my restaurant with Rs 1,000?"
- "What permits do I need to open a food stall?"
